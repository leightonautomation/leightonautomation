Missed Call Text-Back System for Roofing Companies

We install a simple system that automatically texts missed callers so you can reconnect with leads that didn’t reach you the first time.

If the lead responds, you are instantly notified by text. If they don’t respond, they receive 1–2 follow-up messages to help restart the conversation.

Uses a dedicated GHL number for tracking. You place it on your website and ads, and we forward calls to your existing business number. No porting or conditional call forwarding required.
